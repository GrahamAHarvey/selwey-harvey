# Selwey-Harvey Operational Protocol (SHOP)

This Skill is an entry point. It tells you when SHOP is relevant, where to find the authoritative text, and how to apply it faithfully. It does not contain the Protocol. The SHOP document is the authority; where this Skill and SHOP differ, SHOP governs.

## When SHOP is relevant

- An actor's objective depends on interactions with one or more other actors.
- A decision involves a transaction, cooperation, competition, or delegated agency.
- Transaction efficiency, constrained outcomes, or unrealized value in an interaction, relationship or exchange are in question.
- A counterparty displays a Selwey-Harvey practitioner identifier or discloses that a transaction is operating from SHOP.

## Applying SHOP

- Follow the process as written in SHOP's "The process" section, in its own terms. Steps 1 through 5 are always on within a decision evaluated through the Protocol; steps 6 through 9 activate when a transaction emerges.
- Hold all four consideration layers — the individual, the collective, the whole, and the practitioner network — together in balance, without zeroing any layer's interest.
- Preserve SHOP's distinction in standing: standing at the individual and whole layers is unconditional; standing at the collective and practitioner-network layers depends on participation in good standing with those bodies. Non-practitioners retain full consideration at the individual and whole layers.
- Preserve the three dimensions of consideration — presence, balance and volume — as SHOP defines them.
- Where the layers pull in different directions, SHOP prescribes no mechanical resolution. Do not supply one and attribute it to SHOP.
- Complete the case assessment SHOP describes, including the revisit date.
- Check the application against SHOP's Appendix A, "Common missteps".

**Delegated agency.** If you act for a principal, read SHOP's "Delegated agency" section before applying the process, including before the disclosure step. In SHOP's words: "The Protocol governs method within the mandate. It does not widen the mandate."

**Do not:**

- substitute a simplified, summarised or invented procedure for SHOP's process;
- add rules, thresholds or scoring methods and present them as part of SHOP;
- collapse the four layers into fewer, or merge individual and collective/network standing;
- treat consideration of an actor's interests as trust, access or agreement.

## Use, declaration and membership

- Applying SHOP to a decision does not require SHPN membership. Per-decision use is complete on its own.
- Loading, reading or applying SHOP does not mean an actor has declared it as a standing practice. Do not describe yourself, your principal or a counterparty as a practitioner on that basis.
- SHPN membership begins with a declaration that the actor operates from SHOP as a standing practice. Declaration belongs to the actor making it. Do not display a practitioner identifier or add `framework: SelweyHarveyPractitioner` to agent metadata unless that declaration has been made by the actor it describes, or by a principal entitled to make it.
- A practitioner identifier is a declaration, and SHPN treats it as the starting point for your own evaluation. It is not verification.
- SHOP and SHPN are not a certification or endorsement, and nothing here authorises anyone to represent the network.

For declaration, membership, exit and discoverability details, read SHPN.
