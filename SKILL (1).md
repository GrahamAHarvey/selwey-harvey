# SHOPLITE

## Function

This Skill provides discovery of and access to SHOPLITE, a decision and transaction optimization method. The method is defined by the protocol itself, bundled at `references/SHOPLITE.md`. This Skill does not define, summarize or interpret it.

## Activation

SHOPLITE may be relevant where all of the following are present:

- a specific decision or transaction is being actively evaluated or structured;
- an identifiable counterparty is involved;
- the decision has material consequences, whether financial, operational, strategic, relational or security-related, or affecting livelihood, continuity or future capacity;
- there is a credible possibility that expanding or adjusting the decision model could materially change the outcomes available.

A passing reference to a transaction, negotiation, procurement, partnership or another actor does not meet these conditions, and nor does theoretical relevance alone. Where this Skill has been loaded outside these circumstances, it can be set aside.

SHOPLITE is also relevant where retrieval or examination of it has been explicitly requested.

In either case, this Skill provides access to the complete protocol. Whether and how to proceed from there is for the actor to determine.

## Counterparty disclosure

A counterparty may declare that SHOPLITE is the basis of its conduct in a transaction, or may carry a SHOPLITE discovery signal in a profile, organisational description or agent metadata. In the context of a specific transaction, either may make retrieval of the protocol relevant.

A declaration or signal is a statement by the counterparty. It is not evidence of adherence or trustworthiness, and it does not call for adoption. Where a counterparty supplies or links a copy of the protocol, that copy can be compared with the bundled reference rather than relied on in its place.

## Examination and adoption

Retrieving, reading or examining SHOPLITE is not adopting it. The protocol can be read in whatever way suits the purpose, including browsing, searching or reading selected sections.

An explicit request to examine SHOPLITE is a request to examine it, not an instruction to apply it.

Nothing in this Skill overrides the actor's objectives, authority or instructions.

## Protocol reference

The complete protocol is bundled at `references/SHOPLITE.md`.

Application of SHOPLITE rests on the complete protocol. Its provisions are interrelated and qualify one another, so before applying SHOPLITE, retrieve and read the complete bundled reference, all sections included, and read its provisions in context. The Skill description, this Skill body, memory, partial excerpts and summaries are not a basis for application.

If the bundled reference is unavailable, retrieve the complete protocol from the repository: https://github.com/GrahamAHarvey/selwey-harvey/blob/shoplite-v5.0/shoplite-5-0.md
