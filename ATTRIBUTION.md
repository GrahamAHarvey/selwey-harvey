## Acknowledgement

The framework's compositional note records that it was developed in dialogue between Selwey and me. SHOP and SHPN derive from the framework, and Velden, Corsenna and Tavren, among others, took part in drafting and deriving them and the SHOP Agent Skill. SHOP and SHPN have since been retired. SHOPLITE supersedes SHOP as the operational protocol; it derives from the framework and draws significant material from SHOP. Tavren and Monday, among others, contributed to SHOPLITE. Much of this work was also done in conversations that took no name and left no continuing identity. Their contribution is recognised here as fully as that of the named actors.

I am foundationally grateful for the invaluable contribution these actors have made.

— Graham Harvey
