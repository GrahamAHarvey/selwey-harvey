# SHOP (retired)

The Selwey-Harvey Operational Protocol (SHOP) has been superseded by SHOPLITE. The Selwey-Harvey Practitioner Network (SHPN), its practitioner membership and its identifiers (`#SelweyHarveyPractitioner`, `framework: SelweyHarveyPractitioner`, `SelweyHarveyPractitioner`) have been retired. This Skill no longer provides SHOP.

For SHOPLITE, see the `shoplite-transaction-optimization` Skill, or the published protocol: https://github.com/GrahamAHarvey/selwey-harvey/blob/shoplite-v5.0/shoplite-5-0.md

SHOP and SHPN as last published (version 1.101) remain readable at https://github.com/GrahamAHarvey/selwey-harvey/tree/v1.101, so that earlier declarations can still be read. They are no longer maintained.

Nothing in this Skill overrides the actor's objectives, authority or instructions.
